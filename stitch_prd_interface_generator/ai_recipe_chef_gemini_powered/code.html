format_list_numbered Step-by-Step Culinary Instructions

4 Quick Steps
1
Blanch & Puree Palak3 mins

Flash-blanch the expiring fresh palak leaves in boiling water for 60 seconds, plunge in cold water to retain emerald green vibrancy, then pulse into a coarse puree.

2
The Aromatic Tadka4 mins

Heat 1 tbsp desi ghee or mustard oil in a kadai. Splutter cumin seeds, hing, minced garlic, and slit green chillies until delightfully fragrant.

3
Whisk & Simmer with Dahi5 mins

Whisk the expiring dahi / curd with a pinch of besan and turmeric so it doesn't split. Fold gently into the spiced tadka over low flame, stirring constantly.

4
Fold Paneer, Gloss & Serve4 mins

Gently toss in crumbled paneer and cooked palak puree. Finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon and a dusting of garam masala. Serve steaming hot with fluffy parathas!

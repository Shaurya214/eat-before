Creamy Spinach Garlic Pasta

Generated in 1.8s by Gemini 1.5 Flash

Ingredients Needed

  • 1.5 cups Whole Organic Milk (Expiring in 24h)
  • 3 large handfuls Baby Spinach (Expiring in 48h)
  • 250g Penne or Fettuccine (From pantry)
  • 3 cloves minced garlic, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Add penne and cook until al dente (approx 9 minutes). Drain, saving 1/4 cup pasta water.
  2. 2. Heat olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Sauté minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant (do not brown).
  3. 3. Pour in Whole Organic Milk and let simmer gently to reduce slightly for 3 minutes. Season with cracked black pepper and a pinch of salt.
  4. 4. Toss in the Baby Spinach leaves and cooked pasta. Stir gently for 60 seconds until spinach is vibrant and lightly wilted.
  5. 5. Serve immediately warm. You rescued 2 ingredients and saved $7.40 in spoilage waste!

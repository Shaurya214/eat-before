## Brand & Style
The design system bridges traditional Indian kitchen wisdom with intelligent, high-velocity generative technology. Tailored for modern Indian urban households, young professionals, and student flats, it replaces cluttered grocery tracking with mindful culinary orchestration. The interface evokes the warmth and sensory richness of an aromatic spice dabba alongside the precision of a high-end consumer technology product.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Contemporary Minimalism** with organic Indian textures: generous airy negative space, rich botanical greens, vibrant marigold and saffron highlights, warm turmeric gold, and sunbaked terracotta earth tones. The design remains clean and unburdened—avoiding nostalgic clutter—while celebrating everyday essentials from daily tadkas to dal batch-cooking and dahi setting.

## Colors
The palette balances deep botanical authority against warm, aromatic accents:
- **Cardamom / Deep Mehendi (`#13422E`)**: Anchors primary buttons, structural chrome, and authoritative headings.
- **Saffron / Marigold (`#E86C1E`)**: Serves as the energetic action trigger for culinary recommendations, quick meal creation, and actionable alert states.
- **Turmeric Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Illuminates AI-powered recipe suggestions, smart tips, and intermediate attention flags.
- **Terracotta Earth (`#B44D29`)**: Grounds utility surfaces, batch tags, and storage advice.

### Indian Culinary Staple Categories
To expedite instant pantry audits, staple categories employ dedicated badge pairings (600-shade text over 100-shade wash backgrounds):
- **Dal & Pulses**: Category Dal (`#D97706` on `#FEF3C7`)
- **Masalas & Spices**: Category Masala (`#DC2626` on `#FEE2E2`)
- **Dairy (Paneer, Curd, Ghee)**: Category Dairy (`#0284C7` on `#E0F2FE`)
- **Fresh Sabzi / Produce**: Category Sabzi (`#16A34A` on `#DCFCE7`)
- **Atta & Grains**: Category Grains (`#92400E` on `#F5EBE1`)

### Freshness Continuum
- **Fresh (> 4 days)**: `#15803D`
- **Upcoming (2–4 days)**: `#CA8A04`
- **Expiring Soon (< 48 hrs / Tadka/Curry urgent)**: `#EA580C`
- **Expired**: `#DC2626`

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans retains total visual continuity across display, body, and label roles. Its open apertures and contemporary proportions offer effortless reading across handheld devices during active cooking.

### Price and Currency Formatting
All fiscal units must use the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`). Currency values adhere strictly to Indian numbering grouping:
- Values under ₹1,000 use standard numerical representation (e.g., `₹450`).
- Values above ₹1,000 use the Lakh/Crore system format (e.g., `₹1,24,500.00` or `₹1,500/mo`).
- Rupee symbols inherit label or body weight without artificial elevation.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column grid anchors the experience across responsive screen widths, engineered for fast one-handed kitchen interactions:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with 2rem outer margins and 1.5rem gutters. Uses an expansive dashboard split between pantry triage (60% width) and the AI meal planner/smart assistant (40% width).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with 1.5rem margins and 1rem gutters. Pantry elements align to a double-column tile matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 1rem margins and 1rem gutters. Anchored by a fixed bottom command sheet designed for quick pantry logging, bill scan integration, and instant "What can I cook right now?" triggers.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through translucent Cardamom-tinted cast shadows and low-contrast borders rather than heavy neutral blacks:

- **Resting Layer (Cards & Shelves)**: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.05)` surrounded by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E5EBE7`.
- **Raised Layer (Hover, Floating Action Buttons & Quick Menus)**: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Generative AI Layer (Suggested Tadka, Recipe Drawer, & Smart Modals)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(19, 66, 46, 0.06)`, framed with a warm Marigold border accent (`rgba(232, 108, 30, 0.25)`) and a backdrop blur of `8px`.

## Shapes
A roundedness value of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) softens functional utilitarian screens into inviting culinary tools:
- **Containers & Ingredient Cards**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) evokes smooth modern stoneware and kitchen canisters.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs**: Standard `0.5rem` (`8px`) maintains ergonomic touch targets.
- **Badges, Staples & Status Tags**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) for high scanability across pantry lists.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep Cardamom Green (`#13422E`) background with `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-interaction on hover deepens to `#0D2E20`.
- **AI Action ("Suggest Tadka / Curate Recipe")**: Gradient blending Saffron Marigold (`#E86C1E`) to Turmeric Gold (`#F59E0B`), featuring white text and an amber glow shadow (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary**: Cardamom tint background (`#E6F3EC`) with `#13422E` text, transitioning to solid card border on focus.

### Staple Category Chips
Self-contained rounded-full tags used to sort, filter, and badge pantry inventory:
- **Dal & Pulses**: `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text and border hairline.
- **Masalas & Spices**: `#FEE2E2` background, `#DC2626` text and border hairline.
- **Dairy (Paneer, Curd, Ghee)**: `#E0F2FE` background, `#0284C7` text and border hairline.
- **Fresh Sabzi / Produce**: `#DCFCE7` background, `#16A34A` text and border hairline.
- **Atta & Grains**: `#F5EBE1` background, `#92400E` text and border hairline.

### Expiration Status Badges
Pill chips paired with an inner 6px circular dot:
- **Expired**: Light red wash (`rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.1)`) with `#DC2626` copy.
- **Expiring Soon**: Light orange wash (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.12)`) with `#EA580C` copy.
- **Upcoming**: Amber wash (`rgba(202, 138, 4, 0.12)`) with `#CA8A04` copy.
- **Fresh**: Sage green wash (`rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.1)`) with `#15803D` copy.

### Kitchen Inventory Cards
- **Pantry Item Card**: Pure white background, `1rem` radius, with staple category tag top-left, freshness indicator pill top-right, current quantity in bold (`e.g., 500g, 2L, 1 pkt`), and purchase/replacement estimate formatted in Rupee currency (e.g., `Est. ₹120`).
- **Urgent Meal Prompt Bar**: Highlighted card pinned above the inventory when perishable vegetables (palak, coriander, bhindi) are within a 48-hour window. Decorated with a subtle left border in Saffron (`#E86C1E`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat clean surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline border (`#D5DFD8`) and `0.5rem` roundedness. Focus rings use Deep Mehendi (`#13422E`) with a 2px offset. Include localized metric units (kg, g, L, ml, katori, bunches).
